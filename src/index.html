Key Insights
What the data tells us about student performance
📚

Study Time Matters

Students who study 3-4 hrs/week score on average 3 points higher than those who study less than 1 hr/week.

⚠️

Failures are Costly

Each past failure reduces the predicted final grade by ~2 points. Students with 0 failures score significantly higher.

📅

Absences Hurt Grades

High absenteeism (20+ days) strongly correlates with failing grades. Attendance is one of the top predictors.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Education Helps

Students with parents who have higher education levels tend to perform better, suggesting stronger home support.

🌲

Random Forest Wins

Among 3 models tested, Random Forest achieved the best accuracy (87%) and lowest RMSE on the test set.

🎯

Top 3 Features

Past failures, study time, and absences are the three most important features for predicting final grade.
